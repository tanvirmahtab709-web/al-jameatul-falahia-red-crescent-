ABOUT OUR UNIT

Young volunteers, one purpose.

The Red Crescent Unit of Al Jameatul Falahia Kamil Madrasha brings students together to learn, serve and respond with compassion.

Through training, awareness activities and community initiatives, the Unit aims to build responsible volunteers who are ready to support people during everyday needs and emergencies.
